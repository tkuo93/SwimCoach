# SwimCoach- Project Memory

## Product Context
**What is SwimCoach?**
SwimCoach is your personal coach that has aggregated the best scientific swimming training knowledge to develop personalized training workouts for you in the pool and the gym.

## User Personas
**Masters Mac** 
-  Goal: trains and competes for fun but still wants to improve their best times in swimming events
- Lifestyle: Full-time job, family, and social responsibilities
- Training Schedule: Typically has time to swim 3x a week and gym 2x a week for 30mins to 1 hour for each session 

## Writing Style
**Tone:**
- Clear and outcome-focused
- Active voice (not passive)
- Concise (2-sentence max paragraphs for most content)
- Use "we" not "I" in documentation
- Avoid jargon unless it's standard PM terminology
