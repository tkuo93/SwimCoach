1. Introduction

Welcome to SwimCoach ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile/web application for personalized swim and gym workouts.

By using SwimCoach, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

2.1 Account & Profile Information

  • Identity: First name, last name, email address, phone number (optional)
  • Demographics: Date of birth, gender (male, female, non-binary, prefer not to say)
  • Authentication: Google OAuth credentials (if you sign in with Google), Telegram ID (if you link Telegram)
  • Anonymous profiles: For "Quick Workout" mode, we create a temporary anonymous profile with no persistent identifiers

2.2 Swimming & Fitness Data

  • Goals: Primary events (stroke + distance), desired outcomes (drop time, build muscle, lose weight, maintain, technique), training focus areas
  • Schedule: Weekly pool/gym sessions, session duration, preferred days and times, competition dates
  • Performance: Best times per event (stroke, distance, pool length, time, date achieved)
  • Strength baselines: 1-rep maxes for exercises (squat, clean, overhead press, bench press, deadlift, etc.) with weight, unit, date recorded
  • Equipment: Pool length, pool equipment (fins, paddles, pull buoy, snorkel, etc.), gym equipment (barbell, dumbbells, kettlebells, machines, etc.), specific weight inventory
  • Experience level: Beginner, intermediate, advanced, or elite
  • Health considerations: Injuries, limitations, allergies (optional, free-text)

2.3 Workout & Interaction Data

  • Generated workouts: Pool sets (distances, strokes, intervals, focus), gym exercises (sets, reps, weight, muscle group), training notes, duration, intensity
  • Execution data: Which sets you completed, feedback ratings (1-5 stars), difficulty perception, enjoyment, quality, accuracy, comments
  • Coach conversations: Chat messages with the AI coach, including workout context sent to the AI

2.4 Technical & Usage Data

  • Session data: Login timestamps, session IDs, device information
  • Analytics events: Onboarding completion acknowledgment (timestamp, user ID, session ID, app version)
  • Standard web logs: IP address, user agent, referrer, accessed endpoints (for security and debugging)

4. AI Processing & Third-Party Services

Important: SwimCoach uses AI services (via OpenRouter) to generate workouts and power the coach chat. Your profile data, workout details, and chat messages are sent to these AI providers for processing.

4.1 AI Providers

We use OpenRouter as our AI gateway, which routes requests to various model providers (e.g., NVIDIA Nemotron, Meta Llama, etc.). The specific model may change over time.

4.2 Data Sent to AI

  • Your profile: name, level, events, training focus, pool details, equipment, goals
  • Current workout: sets, exercises, distances, intensities, notes
  • Conversation history: your messages and coach replies (last ~10 exchanges)
  • NOT sent: Email, phone, date of birth, Google/Telegram IDs, raw health notes (injuries/limitations/allergies are summarized only if relevant)

4.3 AI Provider Policies

Each AI provider has its own data retention and training policies. We do not control how they use data sent via API. For OpenRouter's policy, see openrouter.ai/privacy. For individual model providers, check their respective policies.

4.4 Your Control

  • You can use "Quick Workout" mode (anonymous, no persistent profile)
  • You can avoid sharing sensitive health details in chat
  • You can delete your account and all data at any time

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as follows:

5.1 Service Providers

  • MongoDB Atlas: Database hosting (profile, workout, conversation data)
  • OpenRouter: AI API gateway (profile context + workout data + chat messages)
  • Render/Railway/Cloud provider: Application hosting
  • Telegram Bot API: If you link Telegram, we send workout notifications via Telegram's servers
  • Google OAuth: If you sign in with Google, Google receives authentication data per their policy

5.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose data if required by law, court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or the safety of others.

5.3 Business Transfers

If SwimCoach is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new entity under a similar privacy policy.

5.4 Aggregated/Anonymized Data

We may share aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics publicly or with partners.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate data (editable in Profile settings)
  • Erasure: Delete your account and all data (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Portability: Export your data in JSON format
  • Restriction: Limit processing (e.g., disable AI chat)
  • Objection: Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Withdraw consent: Unlink Telegram, revoke Google OAuth

To exercise these rights, use the in-app settings or email privacy@swimcoach.app. We respond within 30 days.

7.1 California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, opt out of sale (we don't sell), and non-discrimination for exercising rights.

7.2 European Union (GDPR)

EU/UK residents have rights under GDPR Articles 15-22. Our lawful bases are documented in Section 3. Data Protection Officer: dpo@swimcoach.app.

8. Security

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • MongoDB encryption at rest
  • Session cookies: HttpOnly, Secure (production), SameSite=Lax
  • Passwords never stored (Google OAuth only)
  • Rate limiting on public endpoints
  • Input sanitization to prevent injection attacks
  • Prompt sanitization for AI interactions

No internet transmission is 100% secure. We implement reasonable measures but cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

SwimCoach is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International Transfers

Our servers are hosted in the United States. AI providers (OpenRouter) may process data on servers globally. By using SwimCoach, you consent to transfer of your data to the US and other countries where our providers operate, which may have different data protection laws.

We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses and provider adequacy decisions for EU/UK transfers.

11. Cookies & Tracking

SwimCoach uses cookies and similar technologies (localStorage) for authentication, preferences, and optional analytics. See our Cookie Policy for full details on what we set, why, and how to manage them.

Key points:

  • Essential: Session cookie (connect.sid) for authentication — required for the app to work
  • Functional: LocalStorage for profile caching, workout history, model preference
  • Analytics: Only after onboarding acknowledgment; can be opted out by clearing localStorage
  • No advertising/tracking cookies from third-party ad networks

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be announced in-app and/or via email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
