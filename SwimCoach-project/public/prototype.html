Freestyle Endurance

45 min 2,400m 8 sets Zone 2-3
📋 Workout Note

Today focuses on speed endurance. The main set descends in rest to simulate late-race fatigue. Don't go out hot — first two rounds should feel controlled. You're building ability to hold pace when tired.

💡 Why this workout ▼
Race-pace 50s build your neuromuscular speed. The descending rest tightens the stimulus as fatigue accumulates — this is the USRPT principle.
