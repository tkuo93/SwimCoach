5.2 Inherent Risks

Swimming and resistance training carry inherent risks including but not limited to:

  • Drowning, near-drowning, water inhalation
  • Muscle strains, tears, tendonitis, joint injuries
  • Cardiovascular events (heart attack, stroke, arrhythmia)
  • Overuse injuries, rhabdomyolysis, heat illness
  • Equipment-related injuries (weights, machines, pool gear)

5.3 Prerequisites

Before using SwimCoach, you should:

  • Obtain medical clearance from a licensed physician, especially if you have any heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, epilepsy, or musculoskeletal injuries
  • Be able to swim safely in deep water without assistance
  • Know proper gym exercise form or work with a qualified trainer
  • Have access to emergency assistance (lifeguard, spotter, phone)

  • You experience chest pain, shortness of breath, dizziness, fainting, or palpitations
  • You feel sharp or shooting pain (not muscle fatigue)
  • You become disoriented, nauseous, or excessively fatigued
  • Your form breaks down significantly
